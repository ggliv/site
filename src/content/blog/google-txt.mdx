Most of these are pretty normal-looking, but I got curious about `Z29vZ2xl`. Turns out that's the base-64 encoding of `google`.

```console
$ python -c 'print(__import__("base64").b64decode("Z29vZ2xl").decode())'
google
```

Weird. Wonder why that's there. Some website called [WhoisFreaks](https://whoisfreaks.com) purports to have historical DNS records and it first saw this particular TXT record on 07 June 2024, so that's a lower bound on how long it's been there. The only other reference to this online that I was able to find is [this Russian blog](https://dxdt.blog/2026/08/17/18959/) from a few months ago.

The record could have a perfectly reasonable purpose beyond my comprehension, but I like to think it's one of Google's [many](https://www.google.com/humans.txt) [easter](https://www.google.com/search?q=conway's+game+of+life) [eggs](https://www.google.com/teapot). No Zerg Rush or Breakout (RIP), but still a bit of fun.
